# ADRs

Architecture Decision Records are the durable record of product and technical decisions.

## Format

Each ADR should contain:

- `Status`: proposed, accepted, superseded, or rejected.
- `Context`: the problem or decision pressure.
- `Decision`: the chosen behavior.
- `Consequences`: required follow-up and tradeoffs.
- `Implementation State`: whether the decision is implemented, partial, or pending.

Use `CONTEXT.md` for stable domain vocabulary and settled product rules. Use `docs/product/open-questions.md` for unresolved product decisions. Do not put durable decisions only in `dev-docs/`, chat transcripts, or generated AI notes.
